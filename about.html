Company overview

Dewatering pumps and complete water-control solutions, built in India for the world.

Where there is water to control, Racors delivers the solution.

Racors is a manufacturer, exporter and importer of specialised dewatering pumps and high-performance fluid-management equipment. From 2″ to 24″ pump sets to spares, engines and accessories, we deliver complete dewatering solutions that keep projects moving, sites safe and operations uninterrupted.

Our journey

Milestones

30+Years of
experience

1998

Racors is founded

Racors begins designing and supplying dewatering pumps from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.

2001

First manufacturing unit

A dedicated pump assembly and test facility opens in Coimbatore.

2004

Racors goes global

First export orders ship to contractors in the Middle East.

2007

Wellpoint range launched

Complete wellpoint dewatering systems are added for trenching and pipelines.

2010

Rental fleet launched

A ready rental fleet is introduced for short- and long-term site needs.

2013

Spares & engines division

Genuine spares, engines and accessories become a dedicated business line.

2016

Recognised Star Export House

Government of India recognises Racors as a Star Export House.

2019

24/7 regional support

After-sales support across the Middle East and North Africa goes round the clock.

2021

Exporting to 30 countries

Racors pumps now run on job sites in 30 countries across four continents.

2023

Silent canopy range

Low-noise canopy pump sets are introduced for urban and night-time work.

2026

Full 2″–24″ range

A complete pump range from 2-inch to 24-inch, backed by 24/7 after-sales support.
